Privacy and control

This extension connects Firefox to an AI assistant through a companion application on your computer. It is not affiliated with Mozilla or OpenAI.

Access starts only after you import a locally generated connection file and explicitly consent. The configuration is stored in Firefox extension local storage. There is no developer account, telemetry, analytics, advertising or remote relay operated by this extension.

At the assistant's request, the extension returns ordinary tab URLs and titles, selected page text, links and interactive elements, or a screenshot of the selected tab. It also accepts requests to open, navigate and close tabs, click, fill supported text fields and scroll. It does not monitor browsing or read pages in the background.

Page contents and screenshots may include names, contact details, communications, account identifiers, health or financial information, location and search terms. These possible data types are declared in the manifest. Private browsing is excluded. Text reads omit input values, but screenshots may show visible fields. Password entry, file uploads, cookies and browser history APIs are not supported.

Requested data goes to an authenticated service bound to 127.0.0.1 on this computer and is returned to the connected AI application. That application may send it to its AI provider and retain it according to its own settings and privacy policy. The extension itself does not keep page results after processing the request; the local companion does not log or persist page contents.

Pause access from the toolbar popup to disconnect. The pause setting survives restart. Use “Отключить и забыть настройку” in settings to remove the stored connection, or remove the extension. Removing it does not delete data already retained by your AI application.
